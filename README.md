# Breathe Circle - Professional Breathing App 🧘‍♀️

A modern, professional breathing application with 12 scientifically-backed breathing techniques for relaxation, focus, and energy enhancement.

## 🌟 Features

### 🫁 **12 Professional Breathing Techniques**

#### **🧘 Relaxation & Sleep**
- **4-7-8 Breathing** - Deep relaxation & sleep aid
- **Triangle Breathing** - Gentle mindfulness practice  
- **Equal Breathing** - Balanced calming rhythm
- **Extended Exhale** - Parasympathetic activation
- **Ocean Breathing** - Ujjayi pranayama technique

#### **🎯 Focus & Concentration**
- **Box Breathing** - Military-grade focus technique
- **Coherent Breathing** - Heart-brain synchronization
- **Pranayama 4-16-8** - Advanced yogic breathing
- **Alternate Nostril** - Brain hemisphere balancing

#### **⚡ Energy & Activation**
- **Wim Hof Method** - Energizing power breathing
- **Bellows Breathing** - Rapid alertness booster

#### **🎨 Custom Pattern**
- **Personalized Settings** - Create your own breathing rhythm

### ✨ **Advanced Features**
- **Smooth Glowing Circle** - Visual breathing guide with multi-layer animations
- **Real-time Session Tracking** - Timer, cycles, and progress monitoring
- **Responsive Design** - Works perfectly on desktop and mobile
- **Local Storage** - Saves your preferences automatically
- **Professional UI** - Clean, minimalist interface
- **Scientific Benefits** - Detailed explanations for each technique

## 📱 **How to Use**

1. **Select a Technique** - Choose from 12 professional breathing methods
2. **Set Duration** - Configure your session length (1-60 minutes)
3. **Start Session** - Follow the animated breathing circle
4. **Track Progress** - Monitor your cycles and remaining time
